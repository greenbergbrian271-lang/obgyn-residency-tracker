Programs currently shown, by tier

Methodology note: True door-to-door drive times require a live routing engine, which wasn't available for this build. Instead, each program's tier was estimated from straight-line (great-circle) distance from the origin address, using radius cutoffs calibrated for realistic road-network detour and average highway speed on the East Coast (~95 mi ≈ 2 hrs, ~235 mi ≈ 5 hrs, ~620 mi ≈ 12 hrs). Programs near a tier boundary, in areas with slower roads (dense NYC/NJ traffic) or faster interstate corridors, could sit one tier off from an actual routed drive time. City-level coordinates (not exact street addresses) were used for plotting, since only program name/city/state were provided.

DO acceptance: "DO applicants accepted" reflects the 128-program filtered list you provided from FREIDA (a filter for programs that have accepted DO applicants in past cycles), cross-matched by program name and city against the full 202-program list. Programs not on that filtered list are marked as not accepting DO applicants per this filter — this reflects FREIDA's historical-match data, not a guarantee of a program's current policy.

DO percentage filters: The "≥10% DO" and "≥20% DO" fields are based directly on the two additional FREIDA program lists provided. The 10% list contains 99 programs and the 20% list contains 74 programs. A program marked "Yes" for ≥20% is also marked "Yes" for ≥10%. These are historical FREIDA search-result matches and should not be interpreted as a current-cycle guarantee.
